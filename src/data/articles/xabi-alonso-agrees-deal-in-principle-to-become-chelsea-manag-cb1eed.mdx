- Former Real Madrid manager is Chelsea’s first choice


Chelsea are on the verge of appointing Xabi Alonso on a four-year contract after reaching an agreement in principle with the Spaniard to become their new manager. Alonso, who has been out of work since [leaving Real Madrid](https://www.theguardian.com/football/2026/jan/12/xabi-alonso-leaves-real-madrid-after-just-over-seven-months-as-head-coach) in January, is their preferred candidate.

Talks have accelerated this week and an announcement could be made before Chelsea host Tottenham in the Premier League on Tuesday.
