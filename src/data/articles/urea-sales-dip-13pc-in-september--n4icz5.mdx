KARACHI: Urea sales surged 43 per cent year-on-year, but fell 13pc month-on-month to 611,000 tonnes in September. However, in 9M2026, urea sales inched up by a meagre 5pc to 4.4m tonnes. The MoM decline is primarily attributable to seasonal factors.
