The final is the most expensive ticket on resale for this summer’s tournament. But there are surprising get-in prices elsewhere

It’s no surprise that the most expensive World Cup resale tickets are [for the final](https://www.theguardian.com/football/2024/feb/06/the-2026-world-cup-final-is-in-new-jersey-a-brief-guide-to-the-metlife-stadium). But the next priciest is a group game between two nations who have never reached that showpiece occasion.

A ticket to the Colombia v Portugal group game in Miami on 27 June is the tournament’s second-most expensive seat on the resale marketplace, with a cheapest asking price of $2,254 as of 17 May, [according to TicketData.com](https://www.ticketdata.com/performer/world-cup-soccer?sort_by=get_in_price&sort_order=desc&change_window=14day), an analysis site.
