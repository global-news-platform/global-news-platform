As resistance to datacenters grows, Musk and others are painting a rosy picture. But the US must institute protections

As Americans grow increasingly worried that [AI](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/artificialintelligenceai) will wipe out millions of jobs and create a permanent new underclass, tech billionaires are rushing to reassure us not to worry – the subtext being: please don’t bring out the anti-AI pitchforks.

Even [Elon Musk](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/elon-musk), who recently [merged SpaceX with his AI company](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/02/02/technology/spacex-xai-deal.html), has joined the effort, essentially telling people “don’t worry, be happy” about AI. Musk wrote last month that [“Universal HIGH INCOME via checks](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2044990537145753894?ref_src=twsrc%5Etfw%7Ctwcamp%5Etweetembed%7Ctwterm%5E2044990537145753894%7Ctwgr%5E2b808668231291a94df09f1cc5805ea8c0032b00%7Ctwcon%5Es1_&ref_url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.businessinsider.com%2Felon-musk-universal-high-income-government-checks-ai-job-losses-2026-4) issued by the Federal government” would save everyone thrown out of work by AI.
