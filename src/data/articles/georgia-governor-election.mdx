Georgia's Republican primary for governor has become a battleground for the soul of the party, with a leading candidate who continues to question the integrity of American elections.

## The Race Takes Shape

The Georgia governor's race is shaping up to be one of the most closely watched contests in the 2026 midterm elections. With the Republican front-runner having built a campaign around election denial claims, the race has drawn national attention and significant outside spending from both parties.

## Key Issues

Among the top issues driving voter turnout in this race are the economy, education funding, and the future of Georgia's voting laws. The Democratic challenger has focused on expanding access to healthcare and protecting voting rights, while the Republican primary has centered on cultural issues and election integrity.

## What's at Stake

Georgia has emerged as a crucial swing state in recent election cycles, and the outcome of this governor's race could have significant implications for the 2028 presidential election. Both national parties are watching closely as the primary season unfolds.
