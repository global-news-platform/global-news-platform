- [Business live – latest updates](https://www.theguardian.com/business/live/2026/may/15/rich-list-sunday-times-billionaires-reform-donor-christopher-harborne-hinduja-radcliffe-dyson-beckham-live-updates)


The pound was heading for its worst week in 18 months on Friday as City traders anticipated that the UK prime minister, Keir Starmer, [could face a challenge ](https://www.theguardian.com/politics/2026/may/14/potential-labour-leadership-candidates-streeting-miliband-rayner-burnham)from the Manchester mayor, Andy Burnham, later this year.

After days of uncertainty over Starmer’s future, sterling dropped by about three cents, or 2.2%, during the week to $1.332 on Friday, a five-week low. That would be the largest weekly drop against the US dollar since Donald Trump’s election win in early November 2024.
