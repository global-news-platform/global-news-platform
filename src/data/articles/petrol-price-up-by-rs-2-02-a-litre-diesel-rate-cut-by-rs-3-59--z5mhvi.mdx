The government on Friday increased the price of petrol by Rs 2.02 per litre, but reduced that of high-speed diesel (HSD) by Rs 3.59 per litre. Following the revision, petrol will retail at Rs 391.30 per litre, while HSD will cost Rs 408.53 per litre.
