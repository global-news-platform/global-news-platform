The International Cricket Council (ICC) has fined Pakistan and Sri Lanka for slow over-rates in their ODI tri-series opener in Rawalpindi on Sunday, October 18. Pakistan received a penalty of 10% of their match fee, while Sri Lanka were fined 5%.
