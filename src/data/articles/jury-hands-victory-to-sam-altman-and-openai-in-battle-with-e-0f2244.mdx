OpenAI CEO and president found not liable for breaking contracts made with Musk when founding the startup

A jury ruled in favor of [Sam Altman](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/sam-altman)** **in the culmination of a long and bitter legal battle that pitted the richest person in the world against a leader of the AI boom.

The federal jury in Oakland, California, found Altman, OpenAI and its president, Greg Brockman, not liable for [Elon Musk](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/elon-musk)’s claims that they unjustly enriched themselves and broke a founding contract made with Musk when founding the startup.
