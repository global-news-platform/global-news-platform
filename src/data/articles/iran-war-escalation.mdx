The situation in Iran continues to escalate as international powers push for a ceasefire while military operations intensify.

## Latest Developments

Diplomatic negotiations resumed in Qatar as mediators from multiple countries attempt to broker a peace deal. Meanwhile, military strikes have continued, with both sides reporting significant activity.

## Humanitarian Impact

The ongoing conflict has displaced thousands of civilians, with humanitarian organizations calling for immediate corridors to deliver aid to affected populations.

## International Response

World leaders have issued statements urging restraint, with the United Nations Security Council scheduled to hold emergency sessions to address the crisis.

## What's Next

As the situation remains fluid, the international community watches closely for any signs of de-escalation or further escalation in the coming days.
