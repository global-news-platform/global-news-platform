No one has more at stake than Kamala Harris – who has ‘signaled’ support for its release without saying so publicly

After several months of heated arguments over whether the Democratic National Committee (DNC) should release its autopsy report on the 2024 election, the dispute has neared a boiling point. With [one](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h8IwrO-03WU) recent media appearance after [another](https://www.ms.now/news/dnc-chair-ken-martin-2024-election-autopsy-trump-kamala-harris), the DNC chair, Ken Martin, has set off fierce [criticism](https://www.youtube.com/watch?app=desktop&v=io_tt4MbJmE&pp=0gcJCU8Co7VqN5tD) and even [derision](https://www.thenation.com/article/politics/ken-martin-dnc-autopsy-pod-save-america/), while offering notably illogical explanations for keeping the autopsy secret.

As the controversy simmers, no one has more at stake than the party’s latest standard-bearer. Kamala Harris, apparently [preparing for another run](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/04/10/kamala-harris-signals-2028-bid-00867069), leads in [polls](https://www.270towin.com/2028-democratic-nomination/) for the 2028 Democratic presidential nomination. One of the last things she needs is a widely publicized narrative from the DNC about failures of her 2024 campaign. A maxim from George Orwell applies: “who controls the past controls the future” and “who controls the present controls the past”.
