Pakistan’s Asim Khan has reached the men’s Modern 60kg MMA semi-finals at the Asian Games. Khan defeated Cambodia’s Sopanha Pheng 3-0 in his quarter-final bout on Sunday. The win secured his place among the final four competitors in the event.
