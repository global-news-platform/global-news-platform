Tom Akers and Joe Tanner are finally in the same class.


The two veteran space shuttle crew members [were inducted into the US Astronaut Hall of Fame](https://www.collectspace.com/news/news-051626a-astronaut-hall-fame-akers-tanner-induction.html) together on May 16. They could also have been in the same NASA astronaut selection group, too, had history played out a little differently.


In 1984, Tanner reported to the Johnson Space Center (JSC) to fly as an instructor pilot and then applied for the next class of astronaut candidates.
