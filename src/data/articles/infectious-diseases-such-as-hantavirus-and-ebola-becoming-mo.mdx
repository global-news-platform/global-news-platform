In a move that could infectious diseases such as hantavirus and ebola becoming more frequent and damaging, say experts, against a backdrop of evolving public health challenges. The (GPMB) said in a report that “as infectious disease outbreaks become more frequent they are also becoming more damaging”, warning that pandemic risk is outpacing investments in preparedness and “the world is not yet meaningfully safer”.

Public health experts are evaluating the implications, balancing potential benefits against established protocols and clinical evidence.

Health officials continue to monitor the situation closely, with further guidance expected as more data becomes available and clinical assessments are completed.
