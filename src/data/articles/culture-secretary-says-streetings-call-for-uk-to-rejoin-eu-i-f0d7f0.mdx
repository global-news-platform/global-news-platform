The UK culture secretary has dismissed calls for Britain to re-enter the European Union as “odd”, a day after her former cabinet colleague [Wes Streeting said the country’s future lies back in the bloc](https://www.thetimes.com/uk/politics/article/wes-streetings-rejoin-call-drags-labour-back-into-brexit-wars-3xg53vln2).

Lisa Nandy criticised the former health secretary on Sunday, saying his comments over the weekend risked reopening a debate that was settled with the Brexit referendum 10 years ago.
