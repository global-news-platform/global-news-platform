Many analysts view company’s financial performance as a broader referendum on AI buildout

[Nvidia](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/nvidia) continued its years-long streak of beating Wall Street’s expectations for growth on Wednesday, reassuring most investors that the [AI](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/artificialintelligenceai) boom, particularly the global explosion of datacenters, will continue apace.

“The buildout of AI factories – the largest infrastructure expansion in human history – is accelerating at extraordinary speed,” said Nvidia’s CEO, Jensen Huang, in [a statement](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-financial-results-for-first-quarter-fiscal-2027). “Agentic AI has arrived, doing productive work, generating real value, and scaling rapidly across companies and industries.”
