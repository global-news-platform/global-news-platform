Carlos Alcaraz has been forced to withdraw from Wimbledon as he continues his recovery from the wrist injury that will force him out of action for at least three months during the most significant part of the tennis season.

Alcaraz has not competed since withdrawing from his second-round match at the Barcelona Open last month after feeling pain in his right wrist in his opening match of the tournament. The 23-year-old had already been [forced to withdraw from the rest of the clay-court season](https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2026/apr/24/tennis-carlos-alcaraz-madrid-open-injury), including the French Open, which begins on Sunday.
